Our Design Process

1

CONSULTATION

Make an appointment with our design team. At this initial consultation, we'll discuss your kitchen wish list and the project's specifications. Working directly with you, we ensure that all elements will meet your functional, budget and aesthetic requirements.

2

DESIGN

Based on your brief, our design team will present a layout and cost estimate. Over the course of a few meetings, we'll refine the design to meet your needs. Our showroom kitchens allow you to experience, touch and feel finishes and fittings.

3

PRODUCTION

Once the design, materials and costs have been approved, we produce plans and elevations. A full package of technical information is issued when the order is placed. Your kitchen is then manufactured to your specifications and tested.

4

INSTALLATION

An in-house installation team will project-manage the construction of your kitchen, working with your builder to meet your timelines. From initial preparations through to final installation, every detail is covered.
